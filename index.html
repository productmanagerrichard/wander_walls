Wander Walls is an iOS spatial scavenger hunt game where players place illustrated mural scenes in augmented reality and find hidden objects by tapping them.

  • Uses camera and AR features to place game scenes in front of the player.
  • Displays illustrated scavenger hunt scenes with target objects to find.
  • Lets players reposition the mural and adjust its size during gameplay.
  • Saves local gameplay progress, selected pack, completion status, star ratings, and basic play statistics.
  • Children should use the app with adult supervision.
  • AR placement and tracking may be inaccurate, unavailable, or interrupted depending on device capability, lighting, motion, surroundings, and operating system behavior.

Why does Wander Walls request camera access?

Camera access is needed for AR gameplay. The camera feed is used by iOS and AR frameworks to understand device motion and place game content in front of you.

Does Wander Walls upload camera footage or AR placement data?

No. Wander Walls is designed as a local-first game. We do not operate a server that receives your camera feed, gameplay images, or AR scene placement data.

What happens if I deny camera access?

AR gameplay may not work. You may need to enable camera permission in iOS Settings to play.

5. Camera use and private spaces

Because AR gameplay uses the device camera, you should avoid pointing the camera at sensitive documents, private screens, personal records, or other information you do not want visible on your device. Wander Walls does not intentionally collect or upload camera images, but you are responsible for choosing safe and appropriate play spaces.

  • Do not use the app while walking in unsafe areas, near stairs, near traffic, or around hazards.
  • Do not trespass, enter restricted areas, or point the camera at private spaces without permission.
  • AR placement, tracking, and target hit areas may be inaccurate or unavailable.
  • Children should be supervised while using the app.
